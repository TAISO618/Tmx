About Us

Our story

Our company was started by Ugandans who love showing visitors the country we call home. We began small, with a simple promise: honest advice, reliable vehicles and personal attention for every traveller.

Why we started

We saw many visitors struggle to find trustworthy local arrangements for safaris and transport. We wanted to offer clear communication and fair service from the first message to the last day of the trip.

What we do

We arrange safaris, hire out tour vehicles, and handle airport transfers, accommodation help, customized tours and guided experiences across Uganda.

Knowing Uganda

We are based in Uganda and travel its roads and parks ourselves, so our advice comes from experience on the ground.

Customer service

You deal with real people who answer by phone, WhatsApp or email and explain clearly what is included.

Safety and reliability

We aim for well-maintained vehicles, experienced drivers, and clear plans so you can travel with confidence.

Our vision

To grow into a trusted Uganda travel company offering a wider range of experiences while keeping the same personal service.
